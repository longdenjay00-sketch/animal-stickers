# Red Fox

**Vulpes vulpes** · IUCN Least Concern

## Lore

Reynard, Kitsune, the fox bride, the fox who outwits the wolf — folklore made this animal a mind. The white-tipped tail is the signature on every northern hemisphere fable. Cities did not kill the story; they gave the fox bins and railway cuttings.

## Facts

- The most widely distributed terrestrial carnivore, native to the northern hemisphere and introduced to Australia.
- Typical mass 4–8 kg. Omnivore: rodents, rabbits, fruit, insects, scraps.
- Monogamous-leaning pairs; helpers may stay. Dens in earths.
- Morphs include red, silver, and cross fox. White tail tip is usual.
- Least Concern globally; an invasive predator in Australia where it damages marsupial fauna.

## Known history

Fur trade shaped centuries of rural economies. Introductions for hunting in Australia (nineteenth century) became a lasting ecological wound. Urban red foxes are now a standard mammal of London, Toronto, and many other cities.
