# Snow Leopard

**Panthera uncia** · IUCN Vulnerable

## Lore

Herders of the Pamir and Himalaya call it the ghost cat. A tail as long as the body balances on scree; smoky rosettes disappear against rock. It does not roar. It chuffs, and the mountains keep the secret.

## Facts

- Lives at high elevation across twelve range countries in Central Asia.
- Prey: blue sheep, ibex, argali, marmots. Occasionally livestock, which drives conflict.
- Cannot roar: the hyoid anatomy differs from lion, tiger, jaguar, and leopard.
- Extra-long tail for balance and as a wrap-around muff in cold.
- IUCN Vulnerable; global numbers commonly estimated in the low thousands of mature animals.
- National animal of several highland cultures; flagship of the Snow Leopard Trust and GSLEP program.

## Known history

Western science named it in the eighteenth century; local peoples had lived beside it for millennia. Trophy hunting, poaching for pelts, and depletion of wild sheep cut numbers in the twentieth century. Community-based livestock insurance and prey recovery are the current conservation tools.
